HOW WE RESTORE

Our Repair Process

01
Step 1 — Assessment

Drop Off Damaged Component

Initial Inspection & Diagnosis. We begin with a full inspection to identify damage levels, non-destructive crack testing, and determine the most efficient and cost-effective repair strategy.

02
Step 2 — Engineering

Precision Repair & Fabrication

Handled by Expert Engineers. Our technicians execute high-precision repairs using industry-standard machining, welding, thermal spray, and fabrication processes - ensuring strength, accuracy, and durability.

03
Step 3 — Commissioning

Return in Like-New Condition

Tested. Proven. Delivered. All components are thoroughly tested before delivery, restored to optimal performance and ready to be reinstalled on-site with complete quality certifications.
