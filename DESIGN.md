## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clean, high-precision industrial aesthetic engineered for heavy equipment repair, machining, and remanufacturing enterprise services. The target audience comprises mining directors, fleet maintenance managers, industrial procurement heads, and engineering contractors who require absolute reliability, machine precision, and operational uptime.

The visual style blends **Corporate Precision** and **Technical Modernism**. It rejects decorative embellishments, heavy skeuomorphism, and distracting gradients in favor of high-contrast readability, generous architectural white space, crisp structural alignment, and focused technical clarity. The atmosphere communicates exacting standards, ISO-certified consistency, and heavy-duty engineering competence through clean light canvas surfaces punctuated by an authoritative cobalt blue accent.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated for maximum legibility, high contrast, and industrial authority:

- **Surface Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominates all major section backgrounds, providing a clinical, high-order workspace feel.
- **Primary Accent (`#1D4ED8`):** An assertive engineering cobalt blue reserved for primary interactive states, section kicker tags, active navigation indicators, key operational metrics, and verified certification badges.
- **Text & Headings:** Headings use solid `#0F172A` without gradients or artificial transparency. Body copy uses `#334155` for sustained ergonomic reading, while supporting metadata, micro-labels, and captions utilize `#64748B`.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Single-pixel hairline boundaries that delineate component cards, data tables, and interactive panels without cluttering layout rhythm.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C0392B`):** Retained in micro-proportions strictly for urgent downtime metrics, machinery safety indicators, or subtle brand-heritage pips.

## Typography

Typography is standardized on **Inter** to deliver unyielding industrial precision, high structural legibility, and technical clarity across screen densities. 

- **Headings & Section Titles:** Rendered exclusively at `fontWeight: 600` in `#0F172A`. Never apply gradients, text shadows, or decorative italics to headers.
- **Section Kicker Tags (`label-tag`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`), colored in brand blue (`#1D4ED8`), frequently accompanied by a small square pip or short rule.
- **Metric Numerals:** Scaled large (`2.75rem` desktop, `fontWeight: 700`) to present critical fleet numbers, turnaround statistics, and ISO certifications with immediate authority.
- **Body & Explanatory Text:** Bound between `1.5` and `1.6` line-heights in `#334155` for high-density reading of engineering capabilities and technical specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an enterprise 12-column fluid grid system with a maximum content constraint of `1280px` centered on the viewport.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and outer section margins scaling between `3rem` and `6rem`. Vertical section separation adopts generous breathing room (`space-3xl` / 96px) to maintain a pristine, orderly industrial presentation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer margins. Multi-column metric cards and service portfolios fold sequentially into single or paired blocks.

Whitespace is treated as an active structural boundary. Avoid dense clustering; let technical content, equipment imagery, and specification tables breathe against the `#FFFFFF` canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system achieves hierarchy through **crisp low-contrast outlines** and **subtle architectural tonal shifts**, explicitly rejecting muddy drop shadows or heavy skeuomorphic extrusions.

- **Tiers of Surface Depth:**
  - *Base Surface:* `#FFFFFF` (Canvas ground for all primary layouts and section containers).
  - *Subtle Well:* `#F8FAFC` (Used for technical data card wells, metric enclosures, and table headers).
  - *Active Surface:* `#FFFFFF` elevated solely by a single-pixel structural hairline border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Shadow Profiles:** Ambient shadows are strictly restricted to floating navigation bars or contextual dropdowns: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Card States:** Cards remain resting on a flat plane with `#E2E8F0` borders. Upon interactive hover, depth is communicated through a border color transition to `#1D4ED8` and a 1px upward transform rather than heavy shadow diffusion.

## Shapes

The design system relies on a **Soft (`1`)** shape logic (radius token of `0.25rem` / 4px base, scaling to `0.5rem` / 8px for containers and large cards). 

This subtle corner rounding retains the sharp, calibrated precision of CNC machinery, blueprints, and heavy fabrication without feeling aggressively sharp or playfully consumer-oriented. Full pills are forbidden for buttons and cards; pills are strictly permissible only for micro-indicator status dots or tiny pill-tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-ui`, weight 600), `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, vertical padding `0.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1E40AF` with zero expansion blur.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** Hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text, transparent background. Hover state sets background to `#F8FAFC` with border darkening to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Text Link Button:** Bold blue `#1D4ED8` text with an accompanying directional arrow icon (`->`), transitioning +4px right on hover.

### Section Badges & Kicker Chips
- Compact, monospaced or tracking-spaced uppercase text (`label-tag`).
- Displayed with a preceding solid `#1D4ED8` dot or inline rule. Background is either transparent or a tint of `#EFF6FF` bounded by a `1px solid #DBEAFE` border.

### Service & Facility Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white card bounded by `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg` (8px).
- **Internal Padding:** `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast photographic plates of industrial lathe operations, hydraulic rebuilds, and engineering crews, cropped strictly to 16:9 or 4:3 ratios.

### KPI & Metric Counters
- Big tabular numerical figures in `#0F172A` (`metric-numeral`), highlighted by an accent mark or prefix in `#1D4ED8`.
- Supporting label in `#64748B` (`body-sm`) placed directly below with a structural hairline divider above.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.25rem`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Active/Focus state outlines with `1px solid #1D4ED8` plus a clean `0 0 0 1px #1D4ED8` ring without soft outer glow.

### Interactive Workshop & Branch Selectors
- Modular tabs and branch selector pills featuring structured, hairline-bordered inactive states and solid `#0F172A` or `#1D4ED8` active state indicators with bold typography.
